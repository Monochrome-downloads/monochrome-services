One Percent — Support

Last updated: 11 October 2026

One Percent is an iPhone and Apple Watch gym tracker. For help, questions, bug reports or feature ideas, email monochrome.systems@gmail.com. Include your iPhone and watchOS versions and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

My Apple Watch isn't showing my workout.

Open One Percent on your iPhone first and start the workout there; keep the watch app installed and the phone nearby. Check that both devices are unlocked and Bluetooth is on.

Health data isn't appearing.

Open the Health app › your profile › Apps › One Percent and turn on the data types you want to use.

How do I delete my group data?

Leave the group (swipe on it in Groups) or use Settings › Privacy › Delete my group data in One Percent.

How do I switch between kilograms and pounds?

In One Percent, open Settings and change the weight unit.

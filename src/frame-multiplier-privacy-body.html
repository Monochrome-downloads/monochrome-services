Frame Multiplier — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Frame Multiplier is a Figma plugin that fills the frames on a page from a few master layouts you design — placing, sizing and scoring every layer for each frame's proportions.

In one sentence: the plugin makes no network connections at all, and everything it works out is stored inside your own Figma file, where it stays.

Data collection

We do not collect, store, or receive any data about you or your work. We operate no server, no account system, no database, and no analytics of any kind.

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no cookies, no identifiers.
  • We never see your file, your designs, your text, or your images.
  • The plugin does not know who you are beyond what Figma's own Payments system requires in order to manage your subscription (see Payments below).

Network access

None. The plugin's Figma manifest declares no allowed domains (allowedDomains: ["none"]), so Figma itself blocks any outbound request — and there is nothing in the plugin that would make one. Every calculation, including the reading of your images described below, runs inside the plugin on your own computer. We check this automatically on every build.

What is written into your Figma file

Frame Multiplier keeps its working notes as plugin data inside the file you are working on, using Figma's own pluginData API. This travels with the file: it is saved, versioned, shared and deleted with the file, exactly like the layers themselves. It is not stored anywhere else, and we cannot read it. Specifically:

  • On the layers of your master frames: the settings you choose in Setup — a layer's role (background, hero, headline, logo…), a pin, a clamp, whether it stretches or scales, and whether the hero must keep clear of it.
  • On each frame the plugin has read: a cached description of that frame's layers (names, sizes, positions, text measurements, which image each layer carries — never the image itself), so that the next Scan can skip frames that have not changed; and the plan the last Multiply applied to it, so that a frame you move by hand afterwards can be recognised as a correction.
  • On the document: which page you confirmed as the masters page; whether template mode is on; and, per image, a subject mask derived from that image — the outline of a cut-out product, or a box around the salient part of a photograph. The plugin reads the image's pixels inside the plugin, on your machine, to work this out; what it stores is the resulting outline or box, as coordinates, not the pixels.

Deleting the file deletes all of it. Removing a layer's setting in Setup removes that entry. Nothing here is read by any other plugin, and Frame Multiplier reads nothing another plugin wrote.

Local preferences

Four display preferences are kept in the plugin panel's own browser storage on your machine, via Figma's plugin sandbox: whether the panel follows Figma's theme or your manual light/dark choice, and whether two optional controls (the freeze chips and the template-mode option) are shown. They contain no information about your file.

The plugin uses no clientStorage, and does not access your Team Library.

Import brief with AI

This feature writes a prompt for you to give to an AI assistant of your own choosing, and later reads back the block of text that assistant produces. The plugin itself never contacts an AI or any other service. Copying the prompt uses your clipboard; pasting the answer is done by you. What you share with that assistant, and how it handles it, is governed by that assistant's own terms and privacy policy, not by this one. The plugin then builds pages and empty frames in your file from the pasted text, and stores nothing further.

Payments

Subscription state and checkout are handled entirely by Figma's own Payments platform (figma.payments), not by us. Figma — not this plugin — processes your payment method and billing details, under Figma's own privacy policy. We never see your payment information, and we receive no personal data from Figma's payments system; the plugin can see only whether the current user is entitled to the paid feature or not.

Third parties

There are none. Frame Multiplier uses no third-party services, SDKs, analytics providers, or hosted APIs. It integrates only with Figma's own plugin APIs.

Children

This plugin is a professional design tool and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this plugin's data practices ever change — for example if a genuine network feature were ever added — this page will be updated first, with the date at the top revised, and the change will be reflected in the plugin's next Community listing update.

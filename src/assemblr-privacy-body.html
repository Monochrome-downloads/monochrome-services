Assemblr — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · Assemblr and Assemblr for Photoshop

Assemblr runs entirely on your computer. It has no account, sends nothing over the internet, and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising. The plugins do not ask Adobe's apps for network access, so they cannot connect to any server, ours included.

What Assemblr reads

The images, badges and price styles you place or choose, from your own disk, and the documents you have open. It reads them only to measure and arrange them.

What Assemblr stores, and where

  • In your document: how each product is arranged (its settings, which images belong to it, the badges and prices chosen for the document, its pages and each product, and the file paths of the images, badges and price styles it uses). This travels with the document and goes wherever you send it, like the rest of the document. In InDesign it is kept in the document's script labels; in Photoshop, in the document's metadata.
  • On your computer, in the plugin's own data folder: access to the price folders you picked, so they open again in a later session; your settings (in Photoshop, whether Assemblr may unlock images); and a cache of image measurements and thumbnails, so images you have used before open faster.
  • In the panel: which tab and sections you left open, your light or dark choice, and whether the background image is shown.

None of this leaves your computer unless you share the document yourself.

Purchases

If you buy Assemblr, the purchase is handled by Adobe's marketplace and its payment provider, FastSpring, under their own privacy policies. We receive the sale record they provide to sellers; the plugin itself knows nothing about it.

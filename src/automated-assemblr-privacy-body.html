Automated Assemblr — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Automated Assemblr for InDesign

Automated Assemblr runs entirely on your computer. It has no account, sends nothing over the internet, and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising. The plugin does not ask InDesign for network access, so it cannot connect to any server, ours included.

What Automated Assemblr reads

The Excel brief you link, and any workbook you choose for a table; the folders of images you link and the image files in them; the badges and price styles you choose; the InDesign files or PDFs you place as inserts; and the documents you have open. It reads them only to build, match and arrange your catalogue pages.

What Automated Assemblr writes

  • Your InDesign document: the pages, frames, images and copy it builds for you. Each action is one step you can undo.
  • Your spreadsheet, only when you apply a change in the Changes tab: the order of the products and their copy. If the workbook is open in Excel it waits until you close it rather than write over it, and it writes nothing you did not apply.

What Automated Assemblr stores, and where

  • In your document: how the catalogue is set up and how each product is arranged (the settings you chose, which images belong to each product, the badges and prices chosen, the pages and breaks, and the file paths of the brief, the image folders and the images, badges and price styles it uses, with the matches it found between files and products). This travels with the document and goes wherever you send it, like the rest of the document. It is kept in the document's script labels and XML tags.
  • On your computer, in the plugin's own data folder: access to the folders you picked, so they open again in a later session; your settings; a memory of how you arranged similar products, so new ones start where you left off (you can switch it off or reset it, and its size is capped); and a cache of image measurements and thumbnails, so images you have used before open faster.
  • In the panel: which tab and sections you left open, your light or dark choice, and whether the background image is shown.

None of this leaves your computer unless you share the document or the spreadsheet yourself.

Purchases

If you buy Automated Assemblr, the purchase is handled by Adobe's marketplace and its payment provider, FastSpring, under their own privacy policies. We receive the sale record they provide to sellers; the plugin itself knows nothing about it.

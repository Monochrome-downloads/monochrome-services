One Percent — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Your workouts and health data stay on your devices. The only data that leaves your device is what you choose to share with a training group.

What we don’t do

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs. We do not sell or share data with anyone.

Data on your devices

Workouts, routines, plans and settings are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Your bodyweight, age and sex (used to suggest starting weights) are stored on your device only.

Apple Health

With your permission the app reads heart rate, heart rate variability, resting and walking heart rate, sleep, VO2 max, respiratory rate, body mass, active energy and workouts. During a workout on Apple Watch it writes the workout, with its heart rate and active energy, to Health. Health data is used only on your device to show your progress. It is never sent to us or to any group, and never used for advertising.

Training groups (optional)

If you create or join a group, the app publishes to Apple's CloudKit public database: the name you enter for yourself in the group, the group's name, an app-generated member ID, and per day: minutes trained in One Percent, number of One Percent workouts, training volume in kilograms, improvement percentage and counts of the marks you earned. Health data is not included. Anyone who has the group's code can see this. It is stored by Apple on our behalf until you delete it.

Deleting group data

Leave a group (swipe on it in Groups) or use Settings › Privacy › Delete my group data. Either removes your own records from Apple's servers. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. You can also email monochrome.systems@gmail.com to ask us to delete group data.

AI plan prompts

The app can build a text prompt you may copy into an AI assistant of your choice. The app never sends it anywhere; you decide whether to paste it.

Data Apple collects

Apple may collect crash reports and App Store analytics under Apple's own privacy policy, if you have opted in on your device.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data as above; EU/UK users have the rights given by the GDPR and may contact us or their data protection authority.

Children

One Percent is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its date if anything changes.

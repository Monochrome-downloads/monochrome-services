Monochrome services

A small menu-bar helper that runs certain local, disk-touching parts of the Monochrome Studio Tools Figma plugins — like InDesign export — that can't happen inside Figma's own sandbox.

⬇ Download for Mac
Requires macOS · Apple Silicon & Intel · release notes

Setup

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Monochrome services into your Applications folder.
  2. Open Monochrome services from Applications or Spotlight. It appears as a small icon in your menu bar — nothing else happens, no window opens.
  3. Click the menu-bar icon → Copy pairing token to clipboard.
  4. Back in the Figma plugin, paste the token into the "companion not detected" panel and it will connect automatically. This is a one-time step — the plugin remembers it.

What it does, and doesn't do

Loopback only
Listens only on 127.0.0.1 — your own machine. It never accepts connections from your network or the internet.
Token-gated
Every request must carry the pairing token you copied — an unrelated app or webpage can't talk to it, even though it's local.
Nothing leaves your machine
It only relays data between the Figma plugin, already open in your browser or desktop app, and files/exports on your own disk.
Opens when you open it
No login-item, no background daemon. It only runs while you've launched it, and auto-stops after 30 minutes idle.

FAQ

Why does a Figma plugin need to install anything?
Figma plugins run inside a sandboxed browser context and can't write files like a real .idml export, or read certain local resources, directly. Monochrome services is the small bridge that does that part on your own Mac.
Is this safe to run?
It's signed and notarized by Apple under a real Developer ID, same as any other distributed macOS app — Apple's own Gatekeeper checks it before it ever opens. Source for what it does is scoped tightly on purpose: loopback-only, token-gated, idle-timeout (see above).
macOS says it's from an unidentified developer / won't open
That shouldn't happen with a notarized build — if you see it, please make sure you downloaded from this page (not a re-shared copy), and let us know.
How do I stop it / free up the port?
Click the menu-bar icon → Stop companion, or Quit. It also stops itself automatically after 30 minutes with no activity.

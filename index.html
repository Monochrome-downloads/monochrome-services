Mac app for IDML Export

Monochrome Bridge

The free Mac app that writes IDML Export's files to your disk. Figma plugins can't save files themselves, so IDML Export hands each export to the bridge.

01Set up

Setting up

You do this once. IDML Export keeps the pairing, and the bridge keeps your folders.

  1. 01

    Install

    Open the downloaded DMG and drag Monochrome Bridge into your Applications folder.

  2. 02

    Open it

    Open Monochrome Bridge from Applications. An M appears in the menu bar; no window opens.

  3. 03

    Pair it

    Click the M and choose Copy pairing token to clipboard. In IDML Export, paste it into the Monochrome Bridge panel and click Save.

  4. 04

    Approve your folders

    The first time you export to a folder, click Approve beside it in IDML Export and choose it in the picker.

02Safety

What the bridge can reach

Everything it does happens on your Mac, in folders you have chosen.

This Mac only

Local connections only
It listens at 127.0.0.1, port 7331, and refuses connections from any other computer.
No internet
The bridge makes no connections outside your Mac. Your files and Figma data stay on it.

Pairing

Pairing token
Every request needs it, apart from the check IDML Export makes to see the bridge is running. Another app or a web page can't use the bridge without it.
Reset pairing token…
Makes a new token and copies it. Paste it into IDML Export again, and the old one stops working.

Folders

Folders you choose
The bridge reads and writes only in folders picked in its own picker: your output folder, your Links folder and the folder you save a ZIP to. Anything else is refused.
Where it keeps them
The folder list and the pairing token are in ~/Library/Application Support/Monochrome Bridge.

Running

Only while it's open
Nothing runs in the background. The bridge stops after 60 minutes with no requests; choose Start bridge in its menu to run it again.
Launch at login
Off until you tick it in the menu.
Signed and notarised
Signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarised by Apple, so macOS checks it before it opens.
03Questions

Questions

Why does a Figma plugin need an app?
Figma plugins can't write to your disk. IDML Export works out the layout in Figma and sends it to the bridge, which builds the IDML file, writes it and its images to your output folder, and copies in files from your Links folder.
IDML Export says the bridge is offline
Open Monochrome Bridge from Applications. If the M is already in the menu bar, click it: the bridge stops after 60 minutes with no requests, and Start bridge runs it again.
IDML Export says the bridge needs pairing
The token in IDML Export doesn't match the bridge's, usually after a reset. Click the M, choose Copy pairing token to clipboard, and paste it into IDML Export again.
IDML Export shows Approve beside a folder
Since version 1.9.0 the bridge only works in folders chosen in its own picker. Click Approve and choose the folder. Each folder needs this once.
macOS won't open it
The bridge is notarised, so macOS opens it without a warning. If you see one, the copy probably didn't come from this page: download it again here. If the warning stays, contact us through Support.
How do I update it?
IDML Export shows a notice when a newer version is out. Choose Quit in the bridge's menu, download the new version here and drag it into Applications to replace the old one. The pairing token and your folders are kept.
How do I remove it?
Untick Launch at login if it's ticked, choose Quit, and drag Monochrome Bridge from Applications to the Trash. To remove its pairing token and folder list as well, delete ~/Library/Application Support/Monochrome Bridge.

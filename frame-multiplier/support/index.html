Frame Multiplier — Support

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Frame Multiplier fills every frame on a page from a few master layouts. Nearly every question comes down to one of three things: the masters page, what a frame still needs, or the trial. This page takes them in the order people hit them.

"Nothing happens" / "Multiply isn't there"

1. There is no masters page yet. Frame Multiplier needs one page holding your finished master frames. Name it [MASTERS] and the plugin finds it on its own; otherwise pick it under Setup → Masters. Until it has one, Scan refuses and the Frames tab offers only Import brief with AI, which builds the pages for you.

If you have a page of frames whose names end in "master", the plugin proposes it. Check the proposal under Setup → Masters before multiplying — a wrong guess can be corrected there before anything is written.

2. You are ON the masters page. The Frames tab then lists the masters themselves — there is nothing to multiply here. Switch to the page holding the frames you want filled; the plugin re-scans by itself when you change page.

3. Every frame is already up to date. The Multiply button only shows while there is something to do. When every frame on the page matches its plan, the summary bar reads up to date and the button is gone. That is the finished state, not a fault.

4. Some frames were left alone. Frames that are locked in Figma, or marked Ready for Dev / Completed, are never touched — their row says so, and the summary counts them as skipped. Unlock the frame or clear its status if you meant it to be filled.

"It says updates available after every Multiply"

Click the frame and read the Needs line on its card: it names exactly what another apply would change, layer by layer. Three usual causes:

  • A layer at a different depth in one master. If the logo sits on top in one master and behind a panel in another, the plugin adds and removes it in turn. Put the same layer at the same stacking position in every master, then Scan.
  • A layer with clip content off. The plugin measures a layer's box, not what spills out of it; a layer whose content overflows reads differently in every frame. Turn clip content on, or size the container to its content. The masters rows flag this as "overflows its box".
  • A frame you moved by hand. That is deliberate: a hand-moved frame is read back as a correction and feeds the next solve. If you did not mean it, Multiply once more and it snaps back.

"The hero sits on the headline" / "the layout looks wrong"

Click the frame. The card draws the plan over the frame, and the Placements list gives the evidence for every layer — which masters it blended between, what it was pinned to, and why.

  • "from 1 of N masters" in the evidence means the frame's proportions lie outside the range your masters cover, so it is held at the nearest one rather than blended. Add a master at that proportion — a leaderboard, a story — and the in-between sizes follow it.
  • A clamp on the wrong side. The C cell in Setup → Components holds an edge of the hero at the frame line. sw next to s is an easy slip: a hero clamped west slides to the left edge and onto the copy. Clear the cell and Scan.
  • The hero should keep clear of a layer. The Co bolt in Setup → Components: lit means the hero dodges that layer in every frame; dim means the masters themselves put the hero over it, so it is allowed. Click to switch.
  • A photo's subject is off. Draw a closed path named collision (the outline of the product) or focal (a box around the part that matters) inside the layer, on any master. It counts whether it is shown or hidden, filled or just stroked — only the name matters. Then Scan.

Outlined headlines. A headline flattened to outlines places exactly like live text — once it has its role. Name the layer with headline in it, or set the role in Setup → Components; then Scan.

"Scan didn't notice my change"

Scan skips frames whose layers, sizes and text have not changed since the last read. Two edits it cannot see: a guide path (collision, focal, crop, slot) added or moved inside a layer, and a swap of one master's content that leaves its layer names and boxes identical. Shift-click Scan to rebuild the cache and read every frame afresh.

"Multiply refused — already in progress"

One document write runs at a time. Multiply, Clear frames and the brief's Build each refuse a second click while the first is still running. Wait for the summary bar to finish, then click again. Nothing was written twice.

"My frames are empty after Clear frames"

That is what it does: Clear frames on this page (behind the cog) removes every layer from every frame on the current page — never the masters page, never locked or Ready-for-Dev frames, never print guides — so a page can be multiplied again from scratch. It asks you to click twice, and it is one undo step (⌘Z) if you did not mean it.

"Multiply says Subscribe"

Your seven-day trial has ended. Multiply — and the card's Apply this one — is the one paid action: $250 a month, for studios that roll out campaigns every week. Everything else stays free without limit: Scan, Setup and every setting, the plan and score for every frame, the card and its preview, Import brief with AI, Clear frames, and Promote to master. Clicking the button opens Figma's checkout; the moment Figma confirms, the label reverts and Multiply writes — no reload needed. Subscriptions are managed by Figma, in your Figma account.

Import brief with AI

The plugin writes a five-step prompt for you to paste into whatever AI assistant you already use. The assistant answers step by step; when it hands back the final block of text, paste that into the plugin and press Build. The plugin itself never contacts the assistant — it only reads what you paste. If the review step warns about a row, the block came back with a value the plugin could not read; the warning names the line and the column. Ask the assistant to correct it and paste again.

Privacy

The plugin makes no network connections at all. Everything it works out is stored as plugin data in your own Figma file. Full details: privacy policy.

When reporting a problem, the things that help most:

  • The master and the frame it went wrong on, and their sizes.
  • The frame's Needs line, or its Placements evidence, from the card.
  • Whether one frame is wrong or all of them.
  • Whether a fresh shift-click Scan changes anything.
